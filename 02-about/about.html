ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why Trach?

Choosing a roommate only because you know the person can sometimes ignore important day-to-day differences. Trach is a concept that focuses on lifestyle compatibility.

The four areas

Each person's answers are grouped into habits, preferences, interests, and personality.

Habits

Looks at the way a person lives every day.

Preferences

Looks at what the person expects from a roommate.

Interests

Looks for activities and hobbies that can create common ground.

Personality

Looks at general personality-related answers.

Frontend prototype: This assignment version does not save user data or perform a real matching calculation.
