THE PROCESS

How Trach works

1

Answer questions

You answer around 20 to 25 questions about your lifestyle.

2

Build a profile

Your answers are grouped into four simple categories.

3

Compare people

The idea is to compare your profile with profiles in a dataset.

4

Show matches

The website can then display possible compatible roommates.

What is measured?

Habits: Daily routine and living behaviour.

Preferences: What you want from your roommate.

Interests: Hobbies and activities.

Personality: General personality traits.

Future version

A real version could connect this frontend to a backend, database, scoring system, and real matching algorithm.
